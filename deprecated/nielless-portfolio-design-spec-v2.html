Order. Logic. Amor Fati. These are not decorative taglines. They are the structural constraints the entire design system is derived from.

The portfolio is not a showcase of a person. It is the person. The visitor should leave knowing what he is like in a room, without being told.

Analog personality is never labeled. Nobody narrates their own soul. It is either felt or it isn't.

[02] THE EXPERIENCE LAYER NEW IN v2
THE ANALOG SIGNALS ARE THE MECHANICS

This section replaces the standalone "Analog Soul" page. The three analog signals — camera, Hindustani music, diesel engine — are no longer exhibited as hobbies. They are encoded into how the site behaves. The visitor never reads a label. They feel the signal.

THE CORE SHIFT:  In v1, the analog personality was displayed in a dedicated section — panels labeled "AUDIOPHILE", "THE LENS", "DIESEL SOUL". The visitor read about it. In v2, there is no such section. Instead: scroll mechanics reference the physical rotation of a camera aperture dial. Reveal timing follows the breathing cadence of a raga. The page idles at the frequency of a diesel engine at rest. The visitor experiences the person without being introduced to them.

SIGNAL 1 — THE APERTURE RING NAV REPLACES TICK NAV

The left-edge navigation is no longer 4 static tick marks. It is a rendered SVG camera aperture ring. As the user scrolls, the ring physically rotates (CSS transform: rotate() driven by scrollY / totalScrollHeight * 270deg). The aperture blades open as the user descends — from f/11 (tight, hero) to f/1.4 (wide open, footer). At each major section boundary, the aperture clicks to a mechanical detent (a brief 20ms scale pulse + click sound). The ring is never labeled. Never explained. A photographer will recognize it within seconds. Everyone else will just notice that the nav feels unusually physical.

SIGNAL 3 — THE DIESEL IDLE AMBIENT MECHANICAL PRESENCE

The entire page has a near-imperceptible ambient rhythm — like an engine at rest. This is implemented at three layers simultaneously, compounding into a physical presence that no visitor consciously notices but everyone unconsciously feels.

  • Body opacity pulse: The entire body element cycles between opacity: 1.0 and opacity: 0.992 on a 4-second ease-in-out loop. 0.8% delta is completely imperceptible to conscious vision. Subconsciously, it registers as the page being alive.
  • Background grid breathe: The CSS grid overlay (the dark crosshatch behind all content) cycles between opacity: 0.18 and opacity: 0.22 on the same 4s period. Again: invisible to direct attention, present to peripheral vision.
  • Film grain shimmer: The SVG feTurbulence noise layer (see below) re-seeds every 150ms on a stagger — creating the appearance of grain "shifting" like photographic film in motion. Combined with the opacity pulse, the surface feels physical rather than rendered.
  • Scroll inertia (CSS scroll-snap): Section boundaries use scroll-snap-type: y mandatory with scroll-snap-align: start and a 40ms snap momentum. The slight resistance before snap release is the gear-engage feeling. You push, it holds briefly, then locks in.
  • COMPILE button resistance: The button does not fire immediately on click. There is an intentional 80ms delay between mousedown and the action triggering — the gap in which the sound plays and the border pulses. This is the solenoid engaging before the shutter fires. The visitor feels the click before the page responds.
GLOBAL TEXTURE — FILM GRAIN LAYER PERSISTENT SURFACE

A persistent SVG feTurbulence noise filter is layered over the entire page via a body::after pseudo-element at 3–4% opacity, pointer-events: none, position: fixed, z-index: 9999. It animates by re-seeding the noise function every 150ms via a JavaScript requestAnimationFrame loop. The result: the page surface has the tactile quality of photographic film — not vintage, not retro, just physical. Something captured, not rendered. The grain is never announced. It is only noticed when absent.

COLOR TEMPERATURE ARC — PAGE-SCALE SHIFT SCROLL-DRIVEN WARMTH

The page has a temperature gradient that shifts from cold-technical (top) to warm-analog (middle) and back to cold precision (footer). Implemented as a fixed full-page background layer using a vertical CSS linear-gradient with very low opacity overlays — imperceptible individually, cumulative. An audiophile setting up a listening room understands this: the room temperature changes as you move from the near-field to the listening position to the rear wall. Here, the visitor warms as they descend toward the work, and cools as they exit to the footer.

v2 CHANGE — TIMELINE DEPTH OF FIELD
Timeline entries decrease in visual sharpness as they recede into the past. The most recent entry (NOW → ACTIVE) is rendered at full opacity and full color. Each step back in time reduces opacity by 12% and adds a filter: blur(0.3px) increment. The oldest entry (BITS PILANI) is rendered at ~52% opacity with 0.9px blur. This is photographic depth of field — the present is in focus, the past softens. Not retro, not grainy: the precise optical quality of a shallow depth lens pulling focus forward.
